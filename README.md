# Minimal Home App

A focused, text-based Android launcher designed to reduce distractions and help you stay productive.

![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-Apache%202.0-blue.svg)
![Platform](https://img.shields.io/badge/platform-Android-green.svg)

##  Features

- **Text-First Interface**: A clean, minimalist home screen that prioritizes your time.
- **Built-in Productivity**: Integrated notes and task tracking just one swipe away.
- **Focus Mode**: Block distracting apps and sync with system Do Not Disturb.
- **Usage Awareness**: See exactly where your time goes with real-time app usage metrics.
- **Privacy Focused**: No servers, no tracking, and no data collection. 100% local-first.
- **Customizable**: Multiple accent colors, icon pack support, and monochrome icon options.
- **Multilingual Support**: Available in English (US/UK), Spanish, French, Japanese, German, Italian, and Chinese (Simplified).

##  Tech Stack

- **Language**: Kotlin
- **UI Framework**: Jetpack Compose
- **Dependency Injection**: Hilt
- **Local Storage**: Room (Database) & DataStore (Preferences)
- **Background Work**: WorkManager
- **Architecture**: MVVM with StateFlow

##  Privacy

We believe your data belongs to you. Minimal Home App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All app usage statistics and notes stay on your device.

For more details, see our [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md).

##  Getting Started

1. Clone the repository.
2. Open the project in Android Studio.
3. Build and deploy to your device.
4. Set **Minimal Home App** as your default home app in Android Settings.
