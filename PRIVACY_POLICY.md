# Privacy Policy for Minimal Home App

**Last Updated: September 5, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how the Minimal Home App ("we," "our," or "the App") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your data remains under your control.

## 1. Information We Collect & How We Use It

Minimal Home App is designed as a **"local-first"** utility. All data processing occurs on-device.

*   **Installed Application List**: The App retrieves a list of applications installed on your device to provide its core functionality (displaying the app drawer and home screen shortcuts). 
*   **App Usage Data**: If you grant the **Usage Access** permission, the App accesses system usage stats to show you how much time you spend in other apps (Usage Awareness). This data is processed in real-time on your device and is never stored permanently or uploaded.
*   **User Preferences & Content**: Settings (favorites, accent colors), notes, and task lists are stored locally on your device using Android DataStore and a Room database.
*   **Backups**: If you use the Backup feature, a JSON file containing your notes and settings is created. This file is stored in a location of your choosing and remains entirely under your control.

## 2. Permissions & Disclosure

The App requests specific permissions to function:
*   **Query All Packages**: Used to populate your app drawer.
*   **Package Usage Stats (Usage Access)**: Used solely to display usage metrics (e.g., "30m spent today") within the launcher to help you manage screen time.
*   **Biometric/Fingerprint**: Used to lock/protect specific apps or focus mode settings at your request.
*   **Notifications & DND Access**: Used to sync Focus Mode with your device's Do Not Disturb settings.

## 3. Data Sharing and Transmission

*   **No Data Transmission**: We do not own or operate any servers. None of your data, including your notes, app list, or usage habits, is ever transmitted off your device.
*   **No Third-Party SDKs**: The App does not include analytics (like Firebase Analytics), advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking scripts.
*   **Transparency**: Since there is no network communication, your data cannot be shared with us or any third parties.

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion

All data created by the App is stored in its private storage directory. If you uninstall the App, all associated settings, notes, and preferences will be permanently deleted by the Android system. External backups you created manually must be deleted manually.
